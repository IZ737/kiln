# Code of Conduct

We want Kiln to be a place where people can ask questions, report bugs, and contribute regardless of experience or background. The same expectations apply to maintainers and contributors.

## Our standards

Please:

- Be welcoming, considerate, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Respect differences in experience, communication style, and opinion.
- Give useful, specific feedback, and accept that reviews can involve disagreement.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal information without consent.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized harassment, deliberate intimidation, persistent personal attacks, and disruptive trolling are not acceptable. Do not use project channels to repeatedly pressure others after a clear decision.

## Where this applies

This code applies to the project's GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces run on behalf of Kiln. It also applies when someone is explicitly representing Kiln in a public project context.

## Reporting and enforcement

Please flag harmful comments using GitHub's built-in reporting features where appropriate. You can also raise a conduct concern with the maintainer, [@IZ737](https://github.com/IZ737). If a private contact method has not been provided, open a **brief, non-sensitive issue** asking how to report privately; do not include identifying details or the allegation itself in a public issue.

Reports will be considered as confidentially as the available communication channel permits. The maintainer may moderate or remove content, ask participants to change their behaviour, lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation. Decisions will consider context, severity, and the safety of people affected.

If a concern involves the maintainer, you may use GitHub's platform reporting process instead. Do not assume the repository maintainer is an independent escalation channel.

The intent is to make respectful technical disagreement possible, not to prevent criticism or enforce agreement.
